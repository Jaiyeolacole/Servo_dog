Servo calibration

Fresh off the bench, the four leg servos may not share one baseline plane. Calibrate before the first walk.

  1. Enter calibration mode — all four legs move to a level reference pose.
  2. Tap a pin on the diagram below, then trim it with − / + until that leg sits flush with the body.
  3. Exit calibration mode. Trims are written to flash and persist across reboots.

Do not force a servo horn by hand — trim it with the controls instead, or the gear train can strip.
